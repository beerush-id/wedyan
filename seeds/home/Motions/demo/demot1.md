# Wedyan demo

## The problem

*0 to 2.5 seconds, frames 0 to 75*

**On screen:** An AI in every tab. / None of them talk. / You are the glue.

**Visual:** Windows scattered across a screen, darkened, slowly pushing in. The lines land one after another and stay.

## Black

*2.5 to 3 seconds, frames 75 to 90*

**Visual:** A hard cut to black. The music carries on.

## The logo

*3 to 4.2 seconds, frames 90 to 126*

**Visual:** Wedyan's logo fades up in the center.

## The dock

*4.2 to 5.2 seconds, frames 126 to 156*

**On screen:** Ask Wedyan

**Visual:** The logo's own square stretches into a pill and slides to the bottom of the screen. The W shrinks into the pill's left end.

## The conversation

*5.2 to 6.6 seconds, frames 156 to 198*

**On screen:** Render my promo and open it.

**Visual:** The dock opens upward into a conversation panel. The W stays in its corner.

## The widget

*6.6 to 8 seconds, frames 198 to 240*

**On screen:** Rendering promo, then Promo ready

**Visual:** The panel closes down into a card, and its progress bar fills.

## Control

*8 to 9.2 seconds, frames 240 to 276*

**On screen:** Promo.mp4

**Visual:** The moment the bar is full, the card moves aside and the promo's window slides in beside it.

## Sign-off

*9.2 to 10 seconds, frames 276 to 300*

**On screen:** Wedyan. One workspace, one tab.

**Visual:** The window leaves, the card closes back into the logo, and the line settles under it. The music fades out over the last second.
