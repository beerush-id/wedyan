# Episode 1 · Sent

About 30 seconds, vertical 9:16. Six shots of about 5 seconds each.

## 1. Leo hesitates
*Open-plan office, late afternoon.* Leo sits alone at his desk, leaning toward his laptop, fingers drumming. Coworkers type in the blur behind him. Slow push-in.

**Title:** REPLY ALL · Episode 1 · Sent

**LEO** *(quietly, to himself)*
Okay. Just ask her.

## 2. The message
*Over Leo's shoulder.* The screen glows on his face. His finger hovers over the trackpad, then drops and clicks send.

**LEO** *(a hopeful murmur as he types)*
Coffee after work? Just us.

## 3. Sent to everyone
*Close on Leo.* His eyes go wide as he reads the To: field. Quick push-in.

**LEO** *(sudden panic)*
Wait… To: All Staff?

## 4. Heads rise
*Wide.* Across the floor, heads rise above the monitors one by one and turn toward Leo's desk.

**Caption:** Sent to 1,204 people.

## 5. Nina reads it
*Nina's desk.* She reads her screen, one eyebrow raised, an unreadable half-smile. Slow push-in.

**NINA** *(amused, under her breath)*
Huh.

## 6. CEO is typing
*Leo's desk.* He sinks lower in his chair as a coworker peeks over the partition. Slow pull-back.

**Caption:** Dana (CEO) is typing…

**End card:** To be continued…
