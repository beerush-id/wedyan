# Reply All

A vertical micro-drama in two episodes of about 30 seconds each.

## Bible

**Logline.** Leo finally works up the nerve to ask his coworker Nina for coffee, and sends it to the whole company.

**Tone.** Light office comedy. Awkward, warm, never cruel. The office is on Leo's side the whole time.

**World.** A bright open-plan tech office in late-afternoon light. Long desks, monitors, plants, glass doors at the far end.

**Look.** Vertical 9:16, cinematic film stills, warm window light, shallow depth of field, photorealistic. No readable text on screens; the subtitles carry every word.

**Cast.**
- **Leo** (28), product engineer. Shy, kind, overthinks everything. Grey hoodie over a white t-shirt.
- **Nina** (29), product designer. Calm, sharp, quietly amused. Curly auburn hair, tortoiseshell glasses, mustard sweater.
- **Dana**, the CEO. Never seen; only "typing…".

## Outline

### Episode 1 · Sent
1. Leo hesitates over a message at his desk. *"Okay. Just ask her."*
2. Close on the screen glow as his finger hovers, then sends: *"Coffee after work? Just us."*
3. His face freezes. The To: field says All Staff.
4. Across the floor, heads rise above the monitors, one by one.
5. Nina reads it. One eyebrow. *"…Huh."*
6. Leo sinks into his chair. On his screen: *Dana (CEO) is typing…*

### Episode 2 · Reply
1. The reply lands. *"Bold move, Leo. Nina?"*
2. Every chair on the floor turns toward Nina.
3. Nina stands and picks up her coat. *"Give me five minutes."*
4. She walks the aisle toward Leo as the office grins.
5. At his desk: *"Coffee's on you."*
6. They walk out through the glass doors into golden light. *"Best email I ever sent."*
