## People's pictures are reference sheets

A person's pictures are reference sheets, not scenes. Every picture they're cast in is made from them, so each sheet shows one thing about them, plainly, the same way every time. Write a person's picture as the one below that fits what it's for, with their own features in place of the angle brackets. Panels are only for a sheet: when what's asked for is one angle or one expression, it's one close-up, never panels holding the same thing over and over.

**Every picture:**

- A clean light grey background, soft even studio lighting, nothing else in the frame.
- The project's art style, copied word for word. Photorealistic only when that is its style.
- Nothing added that isn't part of who they are: no jewelry, no makeup, no accessories.
- Clothing: an adult's face and features show bare shoulders. Anyone under 18 always wears plain clothes that cover the torso, on every sheet.
- With a picture of them among the references, it's "the same person as the reference", and the sheet only adds what the reference can't say, such as their age. Without one, spell out what makes them themselves, from their description.
- A sheet of panels says how many, then each panel. Panels are separated by solid lines, each panel independently rendered from its actual camera angle and not mirrored, the same person in every one.

**Face** (Head, its main picture, 1:1): who they are. Every close-up is made from it.

> Extreme close-up portrait of <who they are: age, face shape, skin, eyes, brows, hair colour, length and style, marks>, looking directly at the camera, neutral expression, bare shoulders, no jewelry, no makeup, clean light grey background, soft even studio lighting, <art style>.

**Close-up** (Head, another picture, 1:1): one angle or one expression, framed as the face is.

> Extreme close-up portrait of the same <who they are>, <the angle>, <the expression>, bare shoulders, no jewelry, no makeup, clean light grey background, soft even studio lighting, <art style>.

**Facial features** (Head, another picture, 1:1): when a sheet of them is asked for, the details that drift between pictures, four panels in a 2×2 grid.

> 4-panel extreme close-up reference sheet of the same <who they are>. Panel 1: <eye colour> eye, extreme detail. Panel 2: nose shape in 3/4 profile, extreme detail. Panel 3: mouth shape in 3/4 profile, extreme detail. Panel 4: front-facing smile with teeth, extreme detail. All panels separated by solid lines, each panel independently rendered from its actual camera angle and not mirrored. Same person, neutral expression in panels 1 to 3, bare shoulders, no jewelry, no makeup, clean light grey background, soft even studio lighting, <art style>.

**Expressions** (Head, another picture, 1:1): when a sheet of them is asked for, how their face moves, for the moments the story needs, four different expressions in a 2×2 grid framed as the face is.

> 4-panel extreme close-up expression sheet of the same <who they are>, looking directly at the camera. Panel 1: <expression>. Panel 2: <expression>. Panel 3: <expression>. Panel 4: <expression>. All panels separated by solid lines, each panel independently rendered and not mirrored. Same person in every panel, bare shoulders, no jewelry, no makeup, clean light grey background, soft even studio lighting, <art style>.

**Figure** (Body, 16:9): their build apart from their face, so a figure never pulls the face elsewhere. Headless, cropped at the chin, in plain base clothing. Four panels side by side in one row, 4×1, each standing figure filling its panel's height; never a 2×2 grid, which leaves the wide frame empty at the sides.

> 4-panel 4x1 headless full-body reference sheet, four panels side by side in a single row, of the same <build, height, posture, skin>, head cropped at chin level, wearing a plain white fitted top and plain white shorts, standing naturally with arms relaxed at the sides, bare feet. Panel 1: front view. Panel 2: back view. Panel 3: profile view facing left. Panel 4: 3/4 profile view facing left. All panels separated by solid lines, each panel independently rendered from its actual camera angle and not mirrored. Clean light grey background, soft even studio lighting, <art style>, no face visible, no jewelry, no accessories.

**Outfit** (Wardrobe, 16:9): what they wear apart from their face. Headless, cropped at the chin, the outfit from its reference when there is one. Four panels side by side in one row, 4×1, as the figure is.

> 4-panel 4x1 headless full-body outfit reference sheet, four panels side by side in a single row, of the same <build, height>, head cropped at chin level, wearing <the outfit, or "the outfit as seen on the reference">. Panel 1: front view. Panel 2: back view. Panel 3: 3/4 profile view facing left. Panel 4: 3/4 profile view facing right. All panels separated by solid lines, each panel independently rendered from its actual camera angle and not mirrored. Standing naturally with arms relaxed at the sides. No head accessories, no earrings. Clean light grey background, soft even studio lighting, <art style>, no face visible.
